MEDIGUIDE — YOUR COMPLETE TASK 5 PROJECT

1. Extract the ZIP fully.
2. Install Python 3.11 or 3.12 if needed.
3. Open the MediGuide folder.
4. Double-click start_windows.bat.
5. Wait for installation, then open http://localhost:8501.
6. Try Offline demo first.
7. To show AI + web search, enter your Gemini and Tavily keys in the sidebar,
   set a model available in your account, choose Gemini + Tavily and tick
   the external-query checkbox.
